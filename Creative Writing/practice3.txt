	The student comes out of the stainless steel cold elevator with his scooter at the RTD light rail station. It's another chilly day and the snow keeps on falling on him endlessly. His three layer clothing doesn't seem to stop the cold air from going through his sleeves. 
	But he walks steadily, one boot hitting the dusty RTD stop one after the other until they come to a final stop. The boy moves his not to be seen head left to look at the green flicking LED screen, as it reads out the trains coming. He sighs as it doesn't read his train. Then he looks at the train signal right below, as it stares back at him, showing no signs of the train coming.
	He waits and waits as the cold becomes unbearable, almost tearing through his skin. He knows he can't give up. Thirty more minutes felt like three hours. But he sees the dull train signal turn green, showing signs of a train coming. And he sighs as his breath leaves his cold mouth seeing the train tell him he's here.
